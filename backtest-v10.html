v10 architecture data-derived

Built from IC findings on real silver data at multiple horizons. The five signals below all survived Bonferroni correction on out-of-sample data. v10 forecasts at the 5-day horizon where the most signals reach Grade A simultaneously, and uses regime conditioning (joint-signal radar) to flip sign of certain signals based on volatility state.
Directional core: volume_thrust (test IC 0.279, t=5.10)
Signed by today's close-vs-yesterday, scaled by 20-day volume ratio. Strongest single directional signal on real silver. Positive IC = high signal predicts up.
Confirmation: hl_close_position (test IC 0.232, t=4.19)
Where today's close sits in today's high-low range. Close near high = bullish day = bullish forward signal. Survived Bonferroni at every horizon 3d-10d.
Regime detector: atr_compression (test IC 0.288, t=5.27 magnitude · 0.177, t=3.16 directional at 5d)
14-day ATR / 60-day-avg ATR. Predicts expansion magnitude AND adds directional info at 5d horizon. Used both as size multiplier and as input to joint signal.
Regime conditioner: bb_squeeze top quartile triggers SIGN FLIP
When 20-day BB width is in top quartile (vol elevated), the joint-signal radar showed atr_compression's directional IC flips from +0.207 to −0.293 (lift −0.500). v10 applies this sign flip directly.
Combined signal formula
combined = 0.40·z(volume_thrust) + 0.33·z(hl_close_position) + flip·0.27·z(atr_compression)
where flip = −1 if bb_squeeze in top quartile, else +1
weights proportional to test IC magnitudes
