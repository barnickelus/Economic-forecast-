What this is read first

Prediction markets price the probability of the catalyst itself — the binary that determines whether silver keeps or loses its geopolitical premium. This page fetches live odds and tracks their change, because the repricing velocity is the signal, not the level. When odds move hard while oil hasn't yet, that divergence is the early-warning window. This is not backtested against silver — we have no historical odds time-series. Treat it as upstream context and a logging instrument, not a measured edge. Start logging now to build the history that would let us test it later.

Mapping to silver (via oil — DOMINANT channel is rates/dollar, not fear-premium): rising deal/ceasefire odds → oil down → inflation & rate-hike pressure ease → real rates & dollar soften → silver rallies (bullish tilt). Silver is half industrial, so it benefits when an energy/inflation shock recedes more than it loses from a fading fear-premium. Conversely, oil spiking on a Hormuz supply shock is stagflationary → hawkish Fed, strong dollar → bearish silver. (This is the corrected mapping; the older "silver loses premium when oil falls" framing was the minority channel and had the sign backwards.) A DIVERGE flag = odds moved >5pts/24h while |Brent move| < 1% — prediction market is ahead of the commodity tape. Note: contracts with PAST resolution dates settle at 0%/100% and are not forecasts — search forward-dated markets (June, July, 2026+).
