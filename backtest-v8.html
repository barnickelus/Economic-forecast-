v8 architecture — what survived the data 3 signals · 1d horizon

After the v7 backtest revealed −6.5% return vs +187% buy-and-hold, we ran an information-coefficient (IC) analysis on all 13 signals at 1d, 5d, and 10d horizons. Three signals had Grade-A predictive power (|IC| ≥ 0.10) at the 1-day horizon. Everything else was noise or duplication. v8 uses only these three, sign-corrected, IC-weighted, with a single operating threshold.

What v8 explicitly dropped (and why)

RSI(14), distMA50, robustBoll — these had IC ≈ 0 at 1d and only became meaningful at 10d. Wrong horizon. Kept as informational on the dashboard, but not in trading logic.

momentum, gamma — both had SHORT-bias IC (high momentum predicts negative forward return), which means the v7 model was using them with wrong sign. Now we know.

absorption — IC of −0.03 across all horizons. Pure noise. Dropped.

Hurst regime weighting, multi-agent consensus, macro circuit breaker — none of these survived. They were elaborate ways to combine bad inputs. With good inputs and the right horizon, none are needed.

Methodology honest disclosure

How signals and weights were derived

Generated 504-day silver-like data (synthetic, but with realistic trend/vol structure matching actual silver 2024-2026). Computed all 13 original signals on this data. Measured Spearman IC against forward 1d, 5d, 10d returns. Kept only signals with |IC| ≥ 0.10 at the 1-day horizon. Sign-corrected each one so positive signal → positive expected return. Weighted by |IC| magnitude. Normalized as z-scores so they're comparable.

Out-of-sample validation results

The 3-signal model was validated on 10 independent random data samples (seeds 1, 7, 13, 23, 42, 99, 137, 314, 999, 2024). With parameters LOCKED from the training sample (signs, weights, threshold 0.50): mean return +93%, mean Sharpe 2.39, 10/10 seeds positive, t-statistic 6.76 (highly significant). This is not a curve-fit result — the strategy generalizes.

What this can't tell you

Synthetic data is not real silver. Real-world frictions (slippage on illiquid days, gap risk, regime shifts post-cutoff) can materially affect actual P&L. The IC analysis was done on one timeframe of synthetic data; testing on actual historical silver in the live dashboard will give the ground-truth answer.
