What this is methodology

All previous work was on synthetic data. This page runs the same IC analysis on real historical prices from your ticker universe. Every signal we've discussed (compression, momentum, mean reversion, microstructure) gets evaluated against forward 1d/5d/10d returns and forward 5-day move magnitude — with train/test split, Bonferroni-aware significance, and grading. The "joint-signal radar" section then tests conditional IC: does signal A become predictive when signal B is in a specific regime? This is the WiFi-CSI analog — information hidden in conjunctions.
How to read the results

IC = Spearman rank correlation between signal value and forward return. Range −1 to +1.

Grade A (|IC| ≥ 0.10, t > 3): strong, robust signal. Worth trading on.

Grade B (|IC| ≥ 0.05, t > 2): meaningful but weak. Use as filter, not primary.

Grade C (|IC| ≥ 0.03): marginal, probably noise after costs.

Grade F (|IC| < 0.03): pure noise. Drop.

Critical: with 9 signals tested, Bonferroni-adjusted significance threshold is t > 3.0. Single-test t > 2.0 means nothing.

Train/Test gap: if train IC is high but test IC is low, the signal is overfit to history and won't generalize. Look for signals where train IC ≈ test IC.

What "lift" means

Lift = conditional IC − unconditional IC. A signal might have IC of 0.02 overall (looks like noise), but IC of 0.15 when filtered by another signal (regime conditioning). That's lift of 0.13 — the second signal is identifying when the first one works.

This is exactly how WiFi CSI radar works: each antenna's signal alone is noise, but the conditional pattern across antennas reveals structure. We test all signal pairs.

Why train/test split matters

Without it, a signal that just happens to correlate with the historical period (overfit) looks identical to a signal with real edge. With it, we can separate: a real signal has comparable IC on both halves; an overfit one drops to near zero on test.

What this cannot tell you

IC measures predictive correlation, not tradeable profit. A signal with IC of 0.10 still needs to overcome transaction costs to make money. Most signals with IC below 0.05 cannot survive costs even if statistically real. We surface IC honestly; whether to trade is a separate question.
