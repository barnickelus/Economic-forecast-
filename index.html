How this differs — and its limits read me

What it does: extracts multiple inferences per contract (a falling near-dated peace deal = calendar decay, NOT bearish; a deep far-dated deal at 76% = the real de-escalation regime; a cluster of peace contracts existing = active negotiation). It separates decay from news, reads regime from the curve, and synthesizes the two theaters (Middle East via oil → reflation; Ukraine via haven). Then it hard-anchors on the realized oil move — the one channel we validated.

What it can't do: it cannot tell you if the signal leads or lags price — only the log can. A reasoning layer produces fluent narratives for any direction, so it's constrained to reason ONLY over the live data shown, forced to state falsifiers, and capped in confidence when contracts fight oil. It is an interpreter, not an oracle. The fluency is not proof.
